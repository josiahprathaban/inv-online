A FEW LITTLE THINGS

Wondering
about something?

We’re a message away ↗
Will my invitation look like everyone else’s?+

Your invitation is designed personally by our team around your celebration. We don’t use AI or ready-made invitation templates.

What should I send you to get started?+

Your names, occasion, date, time, venue, and any wording you’d like. Share colours, references, or ideas you love too. We’ll talk through the details on WhatsApp.

Can I choose my invitation’s web address?+

Yes. We’ll help you choose a personal subdomain on invitations.online, such as noahwedsemma.invitations.online. Your preferred name is subject to availability.

How do I send it to my guests?+

We provide a sharing page where you can enter guest names, personalise a message, and create a link. Share through WhatsApp, social media, or copy the message and send it yourself.

How long will it take?+

Tell us your celebration date and what you have in mind. We’ll agree on a timeline with you before getting started.
